DSi-FC-Men – MicroSD Setup Instructions
=======================================

METHOD 1 – Easiest (Setupper CIA)
---------------------------------
1. Download DSi-FC-Men-Setupper.cia from Releases
2. Install it with FBI on your 3DS
3. Launch "DSi-FC-Men Setupper"
4. Press A → all folders + config are created automatically
5. Copy your .nds ROMs to /roms/nds/ on the microSD
6. (Optional) Download nds-bootstrap and put files into /_nds/nds-bootstrap/

METHOD 2 – Manual (SD-Setup Zip)
--------------------------------
1. Download DSi-FC-Men-SD-Setup.zip from Releases
2. Extract the zip to the ROOT of your microSD card
3. You should now have:
     /_nds/DSi-FC-Men/
     /_nds/nds-bootstrap/
     /roms/nds/
     /saves/
4. Put .nds ROMs into /roms/nds/
5. Put nds-bootstrap files into /_nds/nds-bootstrap/

METHOD 3 – Flashcard Autoboot (advanced)
----------------------------------------
Depending on your flashcard (R4, Ace3DS, DSTT, etc.) you may need
specific .dat / .sys / BOOT.NDS files for autoboot.
Those will be added in a later release for popular carts.

After setup you can launch the main DSi-FC-Men menu
(or use your flashcard kernel + nds-bootstrap).
