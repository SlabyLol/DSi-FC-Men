DSi-FC-Men – DSi FlashCard Menu
================================

Place your Nintendo DS ROMs (.nds) into:
  /roms/nds/

Saves will be stored automatically in:
  /saves/

Everything is configurable in:
  /_nds/DSi-FC-Men/config.ini

Themes go into:
  /_nds/DSi-FC-Men/themes/

Boxarts (optional) into:
  /_nds/DSi-FC-Men/boxart/

Enjoy your clean, fast FlashCard menu!
