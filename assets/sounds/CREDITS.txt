Sound effects for DSi-FC-Men
============================

Source: UI Sound Effects by Robin Lamb
OpenGameArt.org – CC0 (Public Domain)
https://opengameart.org/content/ui-sound-effects-button-clicks-user-feedback-notifications

Mapped as:
  menu_move.wav   ← click_1.wav
  menu_select.wav ← click_2.wav
  menu_back.wav   ← click_3.wav
  splash.wav      ← chimes.wav
  menu_click.wav  ← click_4.wav
  menu_confirm.wav← Ding.wav

You may use these freely without attribution (CC0).

The .wav files are in the repo release / local assets folder.
Place them on the microSD at:
  /_nds/DSi-FC-Men/sounds/
